<h2>Tesla Windshield Replacement Isn't Just Glass.</h2>

<p>Your Tesla has cameras in the windshield. Lane-keeping. Collision warning. Autopilot. When glass is replaced, cameras need precise recalibration. Miss calibration by 0.1 degree and safety features fail.</p>

<p>Most shops don't have Tesla calibration equipment. They replace glass and send you out with broken safety systems. That's dangerous.</p>

<p>We have the tools. We know Tesla specs. We recalibrate after every replacement.</p>

<h2>Why Tesla Windshields Cost More</h2>

<p>Glass itself is expensive. Tesla specs are strict—special curve, special coating, special adhesive for camera mounting.</p>

<p>Recalibration equipment costs thousands. Takes two hours after replacement. Most shops skip it or charge extra.</p>

<p>We include recalibration in our quote. No surprises.</p>

<h2>ADAS Recalibration: Why It Matters</h2>

<p>Adaptive Driving Assist (ADS), collision warning, lane centering—all depend on camera alignment. Camera is mounted to windshield. New windshield = new camera position = needs recalibration.</p>

<p>Recalibration requires specialized software that connects to Tesla service. Takes two hours. We have that equipment.</p>

<p>Drive away with safety features working.</p>

<h2>Which Tesla Models We Service</h2>

<p>Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y. All years. All with camera-dependent safety features.</p>

<h2>What Happens If Recalibration Fails</h2>

<p>Camera misalignment stays hidden until system triggers. Then warning lights. Or worse—safety system doesn't trigger when it should.</p>

<p>Recalibration must be done by someone who knows Tesla. Wrong calibration is worse than no calibration.</p>

<h2>Insurance for Tesla Glass</h2>
