## What Counts as Auto Glass Repair?

Auto glass repair means fixing small damage without replacing the whole piece of glass. We use a special resin. It fills the damaged area. It bonds to the glass. It stops the damage from spreading.

We can repair:

- Rock chips (the most common thing we see in Victorville)
- Small cracks under 12 inches
- Star breaks (cracks that radiate out from an impact point)
- Bull's eye damage (circular cracks)
- Side window chips
- Rear window small cracks

We repair all kinds of auto glass. Windshields. Side windows. Back glass. If it's small damage, we can probably fix it.

## How We Fix Chips and Cracks

We use resin injection. Here's how it works:

First, we clean the damaged area. We remove any dirt or debris. We dry it completely.

Then we place a tool over the chip. It looks like a small suction cup with a syringe attached. We inject resin into the damaged area. The resin fills all the cracks and gaps.

We let it cure. Sometimes we use UV light to speed up the curing process. Sometimes we just let it sit for 20 minutes.

Finally, we remove the tool. We polish the area. We clean it up.

The whole process takes 30 to 60 minutes. You can drive your car right away. No waiting. No curing time after we're done.
