## Common Ways Mirrors Break

Side mirrors take a beating in daily driving, especially around tight parking lots and narrow streets.

**Most common causes:**

- Backing into a pole, wall, or garage frame
- Side-swiping another car in a lot
- Clipping a mailbox on a narrow street
- Getting hit by another car while parked
- Glass cracking on its own from age or heat

Sometimes it's the whole assembly, sometimes just the glass, sometimes just the motor that moves it. Each one gets fixed a little differently.

## Glass Only vs. Full Assembly

The price gap between these two is significant, so it's worth knowing the difference.

**Mirror glass replacement:** Only the glass piece gets swapped, the housing stays put. Takes about 20 minutes, runs $80-$120.

**Full mirror replacement:** Housing, glass, and motor all get replaced. Takes about an hour, runs $150-$300 depending on the vehicle.

If just the glass cracked, there's no reason to pay for a full assembly. If the housing's cracked or the motor's dead, the full assembly is what's needed.

## Why Driving Without a Mirror Isn't Legal

California requires at least two working mirrors, the rearview and at least one side mirror. A broken driver's-side mirror kills visibility into the blind spot, which is a real hazard when changing lanes. A broken passenger-side mirror makes backing up and parking a guessing game.

Beyond the safety issue, it's a ticketable offense. A cop can pull you over for it, and it's a fix-it ticket requiring proof of repair.

## Do We Have Your Mirror in Stock?

Mirrors for common models, Honda, Toyota, Ford, Chevy, Nissan, tend to be in stock, so same-day replacement is realistic. Less common vehicles, luxury cars, or older models sometimes need a 1-2 day order.

## Power, Manual, and Heated Mirrors

Power mirrors run on a motor and switch inside the car. Manual mirrors adjust by hand. A car built with power mirrors needs a power mirror replacement, not a manual one, or the wiring won't line up right.

Heated mirrors cost more but earn their keep in Victorville's colder nights, melting frost before it becomes a scraping job. If a car came with heated mirrors, replacing with a non-heated one isn't the right fix.
