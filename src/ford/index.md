<p>Newer Fords have sensors in the windshield. Your 2022+ F-150 might have a camera for lane-keeping or collision warning. Your Explorer could have rain-sensing wipers. That camera needs to stay centered and calibrated.</p>

<p>When your windshield cracks, you can't just bolt in any glass. The sensors need recalibration. That's an extra cost—sometimes $500 to $1,000 depending on your model. Your insurance might not cover it.</p>

<p>We know Ford glass. We handle the sensor work. No surprise bills.</p>

<p>Waiting is a bad idea. The crack grows. Visibility gets worse. Safety suffers. Get it fixed fast.</p>
