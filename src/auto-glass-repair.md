<h2>Chip in Your Glass? Fix It Before It Spreads.</h2>

<p>Rock bounced off freeway. Now your windshield has a chip. Might be small. Might get bigger. You need to know if you can repair it or if it needs replacement.</p>

<p>Most chips can be repaired. We inject resin, seal it, done. Cost is low. Takes an hour. Glass stays intact.</p>

<h2>Repair vs Replace: What You Should Know</h2>

<p>Repair works for small damage—chips under inch size, cracks under foot long. Damage has to be away from edges and away from your line of sight.</p>

<p>Replace needed if damage is big, if it's in your vision field, or if it's near the edge. Also if chip is multi-layered—if you see white or opaque damage inside, that's delamination. Repair won't fix it.</p>

<p>Call us. Describe damage. We tell you if repair works. If not, we quote replacement.</p>

<h2>Why Repair Beats Replacement</h2>

<p>Cost. Repair is $100-150. Replacement is $300-600+.</p>

<p>Time. Repair is one hour. Replacement is few hours.</p>

<p>Glass stays on your car. No removal risk. No sensor recalibration needed if no removal happens.</p>

<p>Repair just makes sense if damage is small.</p>

<h2>Victorville Heat Spreads Cracks Fast</h2>

<p>Desert temperature swings are brutal. Morning cold, afternoon heat. Glass expands, contracts. Small chip becomes crack network in days.</p>

<p>Don't wait. Get it repaired now while repair is still possible.</p>

<h2>Insurance Coverage</h2>

<p>Most comprehensive policies cover glass damage. Repair cost is usually less than deductible, so paying cash is often smarter. Check your policy first.</p>

<p>If replacement needed, insurance usually covers it with deductible. We file claims and handle paperwork.</p>

<h2>Mobile Service—We Come to You</h2>

<p>At work. At home. We come to Victorville location. Repair on-site while you wait. Back on road in an hour.</p>

<h2>What's Inside the Resin?</h2>

<p>Clear epoxy that hardens. Matches glass refractive index so repair is solid. Not invisible if you look close, but strong. Glass won't spread. Water won't seep.</p>

<p>Repair lasts years if damage stayed stable during injection.</p>

<h2>Signs Repair Won't Work</h2>

<p>Cracks that branch out. Damage in driver's line of sight. Damage at edge of glass. White/opaque spots (delamination). Damage in tempered section of side windows.</p>

<p>We inspect and tell you straight. If repair won't hold, we recommend replacement instead.</p>

<h2>When to Call Us</h2>

<p>Soon as you see damage. Heat makes it worse fast. One phone call, we tell you if repair works, schedule service same-day if yes.</p>
